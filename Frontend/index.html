About MediTrace

MediTrace is a blockchain-powered pharmaceutical supply chain system ensuring transparency, authenticity, and security in medicine distribution from manufacturer to pharmacy.The system helps prevent fake drugs by providing secure and transparent tracking of medicines. It builds trust between manufacturers,distributors, pharmacies, and consumers.

Key Features

🔐 Blockchain Security

Immutable tracking of medicines across supply chain stages.

📦 QR Code Tracking

Scan medicines instantly to verify authenticity.

👥 Role-Based Access

Dedicated dashboards for each supply chain participant.

📦 Verified Info

Medicine name & strength

Manufacturer details

Expiry date check

Empowering the Pharmaceutical Supply Chain

🏭 For Manufacturer

Manufacturers can register medicines on the blockchain, generate QR codes, and ensure product authenticity from the very beginning of the supply chain.

🚚 For Distributors

Distributors can track received medicines, verify their origin, and securely transfer them to pharmacies while maintaining full transparency.

💊 For Pharmacy

Pharmacies can verify medicines, scan QR codes, and ensure customers receive genuine and safe products with complete traceability.
